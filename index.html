New in 2.6.2

Unhurried voice guidance

Guidance speaks during visible alerts and rests, with optional snooze and Rested replies. It stops when you skip or leave the page. Pick a moment to see how it is paced.

Quiet mode and separate response toggles live under Settings → Voice.

Gentle breaks

Adjust work and rest durations, choose a full-screen scene or a corner card, and take a break whenever you need one.

230 saved recordings

Browse named recordings with transcripts and delivery tags. Preview, favorite, mute, search, or filter them.

A personal wind-down

Set your bedtime and timezone, choose late-night delivery, and optionally shorten reminder intervals near sleep.

Comfort that follows you

Pick any accent color, switch on a warm tint immediately, and drag the floating timer into position.

Room for real life

Quiet hours, pause when away, video deferral, snooze, and optional strict mode keep reminders within your preferences.

Settings that stay with you.

Six tabs cover rhythm, comfort, smart pausing, sleep, voice, and your daily stats. Scroll to stack them.

Alt+Shift+EOpen settings
Alt+Shift+BStart a break
Alt+Shift+PPause or resume

Local by design.

Recordings ship with the extension. Playback needs no account, API key, or internet connection.

No accounts

Nothing to sign up for, and no generation step before you hear a recording.

No analytics

Settings, timer state, and statistics stay in your browser profile. There are no runtime speech-service requests.

Works on regular sites

Reminders run on ordinary HTTP/HTTPS pages. Only the active tab in the focused window can narrate.

Install in a minute.

Updating: replace the files in the same unpacked folder, reload the extension, and refresh open pages. Keeping the same folder preserves your settings and history.

Built with plain JavaScript and canvas, with no build step. Test with node --test tests/*.test.cjs and package both browsers with python3 tools/package_extension.py.
